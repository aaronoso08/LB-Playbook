Retrofit Helper

A step-by-step guide for contractors and assessors working on home energy upgrades and healthy home repairs. Pick your program, follow the steps, and check things off as you go.

Unofficial helper made by Latino Built staff. It is not from any program or agency. Rules change, so always follow the program's current official guidelines and your coordinator's instructions.

How a city energy grant job usually goes

For whole-home energy retrofits funded through a City of Portland clean energy grant. Steps open and close; your checkmarks save on this device.

    Is the home inside Portland?

    Many "Portland" mailing addresses are really Gresham, Milwaukie, Fairview or unincorporated county. Look the address up and check that Jurisdiction says Portland.

            How a healthy home repair job usually goes

            For repair grants that fix health and safety problems first, then weatherize. Work is usually priced by category and approved before it starts.

              What the assessment looks at

              Findings are written as Problem, Cause, Health/Safety Impact, Solution, with photos.
